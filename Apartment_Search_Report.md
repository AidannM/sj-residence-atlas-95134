## 2. Insider Financial Insight: The Santa Clara "SVP" Utility Loophole

Because **Central A/C** is a priority for you, where your apartment sits relative to the San Jose / Santa Clara city boundary line (Coyote Creek / Lafayette St) changes your monthly bill dramatically:

- **San Jose & Milpitas (`PG&E`):** Residential electricity costs **$0.42 to $0.45 per kWh** plus a 5% city utility tax. Running central A/C in a studio or 1BR from June through October typically costs **$150 to $230/month**.
- **City of Santa Clara (`Silicon Valley Power - SVP`):** Santa Clara owns its municipal electric utility, charging roughly **$0.182 per kWh** with no city electric tax—**57% to 60% cheaper than PG&E**. Running the exact same central A/C in Santa Clara costs **$50 to $75/month**, saving you **$800 to $1,500 per year**.
- **Where this matters most:** **Estancia at Santa Clara** (`1650 Hope Dr`), **Passero** (`2230 Calle Del Mundo`), and **River Terrace** (`730 Agnew Rd`) are only **1.9 to 2.4 miles (5–8 mins)** from 3625 Cisco Way and qualify for **Silicon Valley Power**. Meanwhile, **Vista 99** (`99 Vista Montana`) sits just across the creek on the San Jose side and pays full PG&E rates.

## 5. Questions to Ask on Your Apartment Tours

When you tour your top 3–4 picks, ask the leasing agent these five specific questions to lock in your true monthly cost:
1. **"Is the first assigned garage space included in base rent, or billed separately each month?"**
2. **"What is the average monthly Conservice / RUBS bill (water, sewer, trash, pest, boiler) for a single occupant in this floor plan?"**
3. **"Are there any mandatory technology, smart-lock (LATCH), community Wi-Fi, or valet trash fees not shown in the base rent?"**
4. **"Does this specific unit face an interior courtyard/pool, or does it face an arterial street, light rail, or freight track?"**
5. **"Are you currently offering 4-to-6 weeks free on a 12-to-15 month lease, and can that concession be prorated monthly?"** *(Note: A $3,250 1BR with 4 weeks free drops to **$2,999/mo net effective**!)*
